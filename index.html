What to do when it starts

Drop, Cover, Hold On.

Remember three simple actions to protect yourself during shaking.

↘

DROP

Get down on your hands and knees before the shaking knocks you over.

⌂

COVER

Protect your head and neck under a sturdy desk or table.

↔

HOLD ON

Hold onto your shelter until the shaking stops.
